# Architect's originating notes

Status: **GIVEN research intent; mathematical consequences remain claims to
close**.

This document preserves the human thesis driving the project. It deliberately
keeps the language stronger and more generative than a conventional literature
survey. Formal documents translate each statement into definitions and gates.

## The practical obstruction to defeat

The product goal is a **better perceptual hash**: qualitatively better candidate
recall, deformation behavior, inspectability, and collision rescue at fixed
serialized support. The research must evolve that hash into existence rather
than choose the least inadequate member of an established catalogue.

The target obstruction is the practical conclusion drawn from the claim that
arbitrary truth must be compressed into one fixed-size hash location. We are
not asking one location to carry the entire object. Content, position, and
combination turn “in one location” into two or more complementary locations: a
classical superposition of measurements whose surviving intersections retrieve
the exact external anchor.

The practical goal is comprehensive retrieval over all needs of the system, not
a declaration about every theoretical model of general computation.

The search-engine use is not secondary. The resulting hash should support a
high-quality local retrieval engine and, through exact anchored records, the
construction of inspectable AI memory systems. Kolmogrov supplies structural
candidate evidence; any interpretation or personal semantic memory remains in
the Oracle-managed layer outside the engine's critical identity boundary.

## Concealed-state invention heuristic

When a scalar distance or fixed vector does not evolve compositionally under a
deformation, do not immediately bound the scalar. Look for the state it erased.
The current candidate is the full deformation profile of observable evidence:

```text
what evidence exists
  x where it exists
  x how it is combined
  x which edit histories preserve, move, alter, or destroy it.
```

A useful new hash should be a compact, multi-address observation of that
profile. Its coordinates should inherit an edit/scale law from the profile,
not acquire perceptual meaning only after a benchmark.

## Content, position, combination

Human meaning survives many literal deformations:

> `obersve taht teh maening remainz eevn whne teh letterz change`

The record must therefore be measured in several ways:

- **content:** what fragments, symbols, tokens, shapes, and counts are present;
- **position:** where they occur absolutely, relatively, hierarchically, and at
  several resolutions;
- **combination:** which fragments coexist, follow, contain, cross, repeat, or
  participate in ordered/local structures.

One measurement says “how much,” another says “where,” another says “in what
construction.” The exact breakdown should retain each independently before any
fusion.

## Progressive perceptual deformation

The desired hash changes progressively when its object changes progressively:

- change one symbol;
- insert or remove a symbol;
- transpose neighbors;
- move a fragment;
- grow or truncate the object;
- preserve a meaningful subsequence amid noise;
- retain local identity while global context changes.

Distance should be sensitive to the kind, location, scale, and contextual
importance of the change rather than merely counting edits uniformly.

## Multiple addresses, exact anchors

An object should occupy several useful neighborhoods simultaneously. A query is
measured through the same channel family, searches those neighborhoods, and
maps candidates back to exact records. A collision in one channel is not fatal
when other independently addressable measurements retain discrimination.

Literal equality and exact identifiers bypass perceptual ranking. Perceptual
hashes propose and order candidates; they do not become identity.

## Prevalence

Features have different information value. Common fragments should not swamp
rare identifying constructions. Prevalence must be measured explicitly by
channel, resolution, corpus, and scope. Its update law must be stable enough that
small corpus changes do not arbitrarily rotate the geometry.

Prevalence is not merely one global IDF scalar. Candidate questions include:

- local-root versus global prevalence;
- content versus path/combination prevalence;
- count, document, positional, and relational prevalence;
- temporal prevalence and corpus generations;
- adversarial flooding of once-rare features;
- whether rarity changes candidate generation, distance, ranking, or all three.

## Context and containment

Short queries and long records are asymmetric. A longer object should not be
penalized merely because it contains legitimate additional evidence. Ordered
subsequence and local-path containment form directional measurements alongside
symmetric similarity.

Earlier query segments may act as anchors and the final segment as a target,
allowing retrieval to worm through explicit neighborhoods and relations. More
than one interpretation or source parent may remain when erasure makes identity
ambiguous; evaluation must retain the ambiguity class.

## Banach-space and dictionary direction

The originating commentary proposes that fragmentary hypersphere coordinates
may transfer into richer spaces whose axes represent atomic constructs, roles,
or contextual relations. It also proposes multiple simultaneous dictionary,
tree, graph, and address systems rather than one universal hierarchy.

This is a theorem-development direction. The research must define each space,
map, norm, inverse information, stability property, and failure mode. Metaphor is
the seed; formal closure is the objective.

## Ranking

The current ConeDAG line has already demonstrated useful top-k ranking on its
declared research corpus. PRV separately demonstrates deterministic completion
and fusion of partial rankings through ordered-pattern proximity and prevalence.
The future system may combine them, while preserving original channel evidence
and exact anchors.

## Ambition

Seek the best possible fixed-width perceptual similarity family:

- formally tight breakdown across declared dimensions;
- reliable perceptual distance across transformations and scales;
- independently addressable channel superposition;
- fixed serialized hash sizes;
- explicit ambiguity and collision laws;
- exhaustive reference correctness first;
- maximum practical speed only after the mathematical object is stable.

The intended standard is field-defining work: the strongest construction,
proofs, counterexamples, evaluation, and implementation we can produce—not a
product-shaped approximation selected because it was easy to ship.

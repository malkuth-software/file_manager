## Rules

- Split a claim when its assumptions or closure methods differ.
- Never upgrade **HYPOTHESIS** directly to **DECIDED**; establish a proof or
  measurement status first.
- Record counterexamples as progress. A failed universal claim often yields the
  correct restricted theorem.

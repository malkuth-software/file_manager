# Prior-art boundary 001: support, flow, scale, and interaction

Status: **OBSERVED scoped primary-source audit; not an exhaustive novelty or
patent search**.

Date: 2026-08-05.

## Outcome

The individual mathematical ingredients of the current support program are not
novel candidates. Minimal predictive partitions, flow equivalence, approximate
bisimulation, relevant-information compression, protected graph coloring,
tensor interaction decomposition, four-cell fixed-margin moves,
coarse/detail refinement, nested prefixes, ordered-subsequence features, and
edit sketches all have established neighboring literatures.

Novelty language must therefore attach, if at all, to a narrower integrated
construction after a deeper audit. No current Kolmogrov theorem is promoted to
novel by this review.

## Claims now excluded

Do not claim novelty for:

- minimum predictive or lumpable state partitions;
- approximate bisimulation/state aggregation;
- graph coloring as zero-error support;
- mutual information as task relevance;
- tensor main/interaction decomposition;
- four-cell fixed-margin interaction moves;
- orthogonal multiresolution coarse/detail decomposition;
- useful embedding prefixes or successive refinement;
- gapped ordered-subsequence features;
- multiple hash tables, product addresses, or edit-distance sketches.

These remain valid controls and dependencies.

## Narrow constructions still unverified

The scoped sources did not establish these exact combinations:

1. the canonical gap-simplex occurrence measure together with the exact
   deletion split into witness destruction and standard-basis gap transport,
   plus the substitution/transposition incidence split;
2. selecting modular gap-address interaction cycles from least collision
   obstructions while preserving independent evidence channels;
3. combining flow-closed support, protected obstruction width, affine-direction
   ledgers, and interval-certified quantized ancestry in one inspectable
   fixed-width retrieval candidate;
4. exporting that construction as independently addressable evidence with exact
   catalogue verification and versioned migration under ORC-KOL-001.

These are **UNVERIFIED**, not novel. Absence from this small source set is not
evidence of absence from the literature.

## Rules

- A result may instantiate one row without selecting the others.
- No measured threshold is retroactively declared after inspecting failures.
- If `Phi_m` is only approximately projective, its residual response is a
  separate channel in every Jacobian and energy report.
- A finite no-collision result does not close S004, S010, or S012.
- “Minimum support” in a claim must cite the support row from
  `SUPPORT_SCALE_JACOBIAN_PROGRAM.md` and the assumptions used here.

## Constraint synthesis

- **GIVEN:** the house voice is an unplaceable, high-definition 1998–2007
  information instrument with Encarta '00 richness, vaporwave atmosphere,
  professional seriousness, and clicky physical depth.
- **GIVEN:** foreground/background relations are primary. Color does not replace
  physical-role depth.
- **GIVEN:** the central file field remains white, warm pearl, or nearly
  imperceptibly tinted. Vaporwave color is concentrated in the title fresco,
  selection relation, object icons, focus, and rare signals.
- **GIVEN:** geometry and density remain constant across authored themes.
- **GIVEN:** retain these twelve atmospheres within one house system and default
  to Sapphire Dusk. They are an axis independent of structural styling, not
  twelve independent products.

## Shared styling rules

Every family preserves the same **CANDIDATE** material stack:

1. one upper-left global light;
2. title fresco made from a three-stop structural gradient plus three broad,
   low-frequency atmospheric blooms;
3. raised command plates with a bright top keyline and darker lower edge;
4. inset path/search/edit wells with a local occlusion shadow;
5. deeper borders only at actual plane boundaries;
6. semantic selection as a background/foreground/focus relationship;
7. white or pearl file canvas with no colored card wallpaper;
8. high-color object icons independent of the theme's chrome tint.

Theme changes may alter atmosphere and paired color roles. They may not alter
spacing, pane topology, control affordance, evidence meaning, or filesystem
authority.

## Approved comparison set

**GIVEN:** all twelve remain available in the review instrument and Sapphire
Dusk is the initial/default atmosphere. The earlier four-family comparison is
retained as a useful spread, not a pending selection:

- **Encarta Cobalt** — continuity and default-house plausibility;

- **Phosphor Violet** — maximum industrial seriousness.

alternate emphases, not discarded work.

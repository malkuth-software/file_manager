## Cross-family intensity questions

- **V191** — Should the main browsing window be 90% calm utility / 10% jewel,
  75/25, or richer?
- **V192** — May title-bar fresco vary subtly by location/type, or must it be one
  stable identity texture?
- **V193** — Are beveled controls always visible, visible only on hover/focus, or
  dependent on control importance?
- **V194** — Should empty white space remain truly white, warm pearl, or carry a
  nearly imperceptible Watercolor field?
- **V195** — Should icon materials be consistent across all file types, or may
  rare types have deliberately eccentric handcrafted objects?
- **V196** — May index/search operations have bespoke ambient animations, or
  should animation be reserved for installation and rare long tasks?
- **V197** — Should click sounds identify the control material, the action
  category, or both?
- **V198** — Do we offer one house style only, or Watercolor and Ergonomic house
  variants sharing geometry and metrics?

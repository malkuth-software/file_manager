## Material doctrine

The result is not “light skeuomorphism.” GUI.Forms may use a complete and
systematic physical grammar:

- one global light direction;
- raised action plates and inset edit wells;
- precise two-step edges;
- etched semantic groupings;
- visibly mechanical spinner actuators;
- distinct disabled and default-action depth;
- deeper instruments embedded inside shallower command structure;
- and window/plane shadows where they establish real layering.

The Windows 95 appearance family (`M101`–`M105`) is accepted in full. Depth is
therefore an architectural styling primitive, not decoration painted onto a
flat widget system.

## Configuration surfaces

- Literal, high-color object icons are accepted, as are centered two-line
  labels, precise dotted keyboard focus, neutral chrome, and compact status.
- Objects floating without environmental structure are rejected (`M012`). An
  object field needs grounding through geometry, grouping, or an authored
  surface rather than modern cards or complete visual weightlessness.
- Analog instruments may coexist with exact editable values.
- Appearance configuration should use a miniature UI diorama capable of showing
  active, inactive, selected, message, scrollbar, and typography states at
  once. Authored schemes may bundle color, font, icon, pointer, and sound while
  exposing which members will be applied.
- The Windows Me object/label/description row, compact help column, corner
  illustration, edge bleed, and factual descriptions are accepted. Browser-blue
  underlined link styling is rejected.

## Professional custom surfaces

- SkyOS's shallow shadows, rounded internal tabs, pearl-grey bounded planes,
  and fully custom conventional behavior are accepted.
- Large calm margins are rejected. GUI.Forms should be composed and breathable,
  not spacious for its own sake.
- Tiny colored icons in long technical lists require distillation; icon-only
  tool strips are rejected.
- A black measurement well inside a light utility is accepted, with one
  phosphor-like color reserved for live data. Instrument depth may exceed
  command depth.
- Neutral grey selection is rejected; selection remains a deliberate semantic
  color role.

## Boundary cases

- A charcoal transient menu with soft corners, a mild gradient, explicit
  boundaries, and clear hover/focus is within bounds. Dark primary application
  surfaces remain outside the direction.
- Solus-style category/place columns are accepted. Its bottom-integrated search
  field and high-color icons floating on a near-flat plane are rejected.
- AOL-level toolbar personality is accepted: distinct labelled destination
  objects over plain utility machinery. The purple structural rail and cyan
  modal brand plate are rejected. Color-family segmentation must be distilled.
- JumpStart contributes continuous instrument housings, physically distinct
  displays/buttons/meters, an unequivocal physical pointer, and diegetic
  clarity. Stacks of command plates, cartoon-scale surface noise, and strong
  local rainbow materials do not survive professionalization.

## Frozen procedural pattern arsenal

- **Pipes:** accepted completely—glossy tubular material, independent colors,
  right-angle procedural networks, and D4-only/bounded placement.
- **Maze:** accepted completely—including literal room/corridor imagery,
  synthetic material collisions, low-poly architecture, and the period's
  imperfect texture-mapping character. Distillation is still required for HD.
- **Flower Box:** only the rare exceptional-object principle survives. The
  single object requires adaptation; violent adjacent-face colors and black
  negative space are rejected. It remains easter-egg-level.
- **Flying Windows:** repeated house-object depth and a File Manager house glyph
  are accepted. Sparse multicolor objects on black are rejected; repetition and
  branding must be adapted.

## Consequent depth map

| Locus | Default depth |
|---|---|
| Data/content | calm plane with explicit selection and object grounding |
| Input | inset well with clear focus and editing boundary |
| Action | shallow raised plate with physical press state |
| Grouping | etched or geometrically bounded; not card-based |
| Instrument | deeper well; may use specialized dark/live-data material |
| Window/popup | shadow and plane separation proportional to actual stacking |
